# Pharmacy-Sales-Drug-analysis-Using-Excel
Analyzed pharmacy sales data using Microsoft Excel to identify monthly revenue trends, top-selling medicines, branded vs. generic sales, category-wise revenue, and payment preferences using PivotTables, PivotCharts, and dashboard visualization.

## 📌 Project Overview

The **Pharmacy Sales & Drug Analysis** project focuses on analyzing pharmacy sales data to identify medicine sales trends, revenue patterns, and customer payment preferences using Microsoft Excel.

The project uses sales data from January to December 2025 to evaluate top-selling medicines, monthly revenue trends, branded versus generic medicine performance, and revenue contribution by therapeutic category.

The analysis was performed using Excel formulas, PivotTables, PivotCharts, and an interactive-style dashboard to transform raw sales data into meaningful insights.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze monthly pharmacy revenue and identify sales trends.
- Identify the top five medicines based on revenue.
- Compare branded and generic medicines by revenue and units sold.
- Evaluate revenue contribution across medicine categories.
- Analyze payment methods based on revenue.
- Develop a dashboard to present key findings visually.

## 📉 Key Analysis and Findings

### 1. Monthly Revenue Analysis

- **Highest monthly revenue:** September — ₹35,600.
- **Lowest monthly revenue:** April — approximately ₹27,233.
- Monthly revenue varied throughout the year, highlighting fluctuations in pharmacy sales performance.

### 2. Branded vs. Generic Medicines

**Revenue contribution:**
- Branded medicines: 81.4%
- Generic medicines: 18.6%

**Units sold:**
- Branded medicines: 59.6%
- Generic medicines: 40.4%

The comparison shows that branded medicines contributed a larger share of total revenue, while generic medicines accounted for a substantial proportion of units sold.

### 3. Top Five Medicines by Revenue

| Medicine | Revenue |
|---|---:|
| Augmentin 625 | ₹39.1K |
| Telma 40 | ₹34.9K |
| Amaryl 2 | ₹33.2K |
| Pan 40 | ₹29.7K |
| Atorva 10 | ₹25.5K |

**Key finding:** Augmentin 625 recorded the highest revenue among the five medicines analyzed.

### 4. Revenue by Medicine Category

| Category | Revenue |
|---|---:|
| Antibiotic | ₹74.7K |
| Cardiac/Lipid | ₹63.8K |
| Antihypertensive | ₹58.7K |
| Antidiabetic | ₹55.1K |
| Gastrointestinal | ₹44.8K |
| Analgesic/Antipyretic | ₹34.7K |

**Key finding:** Antibiotics generated the highest revenue among the categories displayed.

### 5. Payment Mode Analysis

| Payment Mode | Revenue Share |
|---|---:|
| UPI | 49.3% |
| Cash | 17.5% |
| Card | 17.0% |
| Insurance | 16.3% |

**Key finding:** UPI was the leading payment mode, accounting for 49.3% of total revenue.

## 💡 Business Insights

The analysis highlights several useful patterns:

- September recorded the highest monthly revenue, while April recorded the lowest.
- Branded medicines generated a higher proportion of revenue than generic medicines.
- Generic medicines represented 40.4% of units sold but contributed 18.6% of revenue.
- Augmentin 625 was the highest-revenue medicine among the top five products.
- Antibiotics contributed the highest revenue among the six categories analyzed.
- UPI was the most frequently represented revenue payment mode in the dashboard.

These findings can support further investigation into inventory planning, medicine demand, sales performance, and payment preferences.
